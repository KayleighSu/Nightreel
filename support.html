NIGHTREEL SUPPORT

Need a hand?

For questions about Nightreel, account access, artwork removal, privacy or bugs, write to:

support.nightreel@gmail.com

Reporting harmful content

For a user you have friended, open their profile and choose Report profile or artwork to submit a private report for administrator review. You can also block the account. For concerns you cannot submit in the app, email us with relevant details; avoid including passwords or other sensitive information.

Reporting chat messages

Inside a Nightreel conversation, select Report on the specific message you received, then describe the concern. Your report shares the flagged message and up to six nearby messages from that conversation with authorized moderators. The other account is not automatically penalized. You can also report a profile or block a user.

Appealing moderation actions

If your account was suspended or your profile artwork removed, open your Nightreel profile menu and choose My moderation appeals. You can explain your situation and optionally select up to eight relevant messages for a moderator to review. One appeal is available per action within 90 days. If you cannot sign in because of a suspension, send an appeal from your registered email to support.nightreel@gmail.com, describing the action and attaching any relevant evidence. Do not send passwords. Responses and review times are not guaranteed.

Copyright and artwork

If you believe an image or other content on Nightreel infringes your rights, send the URL or other identifying details, your contact information, and a description of your concern. We review reports and removal requests. This email is a general contact and should not be assumed to be a registered DMCA designated agent.

Delete an account

In Nightreel, open your profile menu, expand Account and data deletion, and follow the permanent deletion prompts. If the flow is unavailable or fails, contact support using the email above.

Support inquiries are not monitored in real time. For emergencies, use local emergency resources.
