PRIVACY NOTICE · NOVEMBER 20, 2026

Your library. Your privacy.

This notice describes how Nightreel handles information when you create an account, build an anime library, connect with friends or send messages. Nightreel is operated as a user-created anime tracking and social application. For privacy questions, email support.nightreel@gmail.com.

Information involved in using Nightreel

  • Account information: Your email address and account identifier are used for registration, authentication and account recovery. Authentication is provided by Supabase Auth; Nightreel does not store your account password in its anime library database.
  • Public or friend-visible profile details: Your username, display name and optional profile images or banner. Account lookup can display usernames and display names to other signed-in users.
  • Your anime collection: Titles, viewing status, episode progress, favorites, ratings, artwork and notes. Personal notes are separate from optional notes shared with accepted friends. Accepted friends can see the library information Nightreel exposes through its friend-view features.
  • Social features: Friend requests and accepted connections, direct messages, blocks, and reports submitted about other users or content. Messages are intended for participants; administrators may access records as needed to operate the service and respond to lawful requests or safety issues.
  • Uploaded images: You may upload cover artwork, avatars or profile banners. Cover artwork is kept in a public storage bucket and anyone with its URL may be able to view it. Avatars and banners are stored in private buckets with access restrictions, but authorized users may receive temporary URLs to display them.
  • Technical information: Hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical information, such as IP addresses, timestamps, device/browser details, request metadata and security logs, to deliver and protect the service.

Why the information is used

Nightreel uses this information to create and protect accounts; save and synchronize anime libraries; display permitted friend activity; deliver messages; support uploaded images; investigate reports and abuse; maintain application reliability; and answer support requests.

Providers and outside sources

Nightreel uses Supabase for authentication, database storage, image storage and server functions, and GitHub Pages to host the website. Anime search and metadata can come from services such as Jikan/MyAnimeList and AniList; metadata lookups may send anime titles or identifiers to those services. Opening external anime or streaming links may subject you to those websites' privacy practices.

Who can see information

Your username and display name may be discoverable to other signed-in users. Accepted friends may see collection and profile details exposed by Nightreel's friend features. Messages are subject to participant access controls. Reports can be read by their submitter and authorized Nightreel moderators. Public cover-artwork URLs may be accessible more broadly. We do not describe every piece of user content as private.

Camera, photos and permissions

Nightreel requests camera access only when you start scanning a friend's QR code. Choosing a photo or artwork for upload opens the device's file or photo picker. You can deny camera access and still use other Nightreel features.

Storage and deletion

Your information is stored while your account and relevant content remain active, subject to infrastructure, security, backup and legal retention requirements. You can edit or remove anime entries and optional images through available account features. To permanently delete your account, open Nightreel's profile menu, select Account and data deletion and follow the confirmation steps. Deletion is intended to remove account-linked library, message, friendship, profile and uploaded-image records, but technical logs, backups, and information required for safety or legal purposes may be retained for limited periods. Contact support if deletion fails.

Security and choices

Nightreel uses Supabase authentication, row-level database authorization and restricted storage permissions. No online service can guarantee absolute security. You can choose whether to add friends, share public notes, upload images or grant camera access. You can block people and report concerns.

Children

Nightreel is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact support to request review and removal.

Updates and contact

This notice may be updated as Nightreel changes. The revised version will be published here with its effective date. For account, privacy, removal and safety inquiries: support.nightreel@gmail.com.

This notice describes currently inspected app behavior and should undergo final operator/legal review, particularly for retention practices, applicable privacy laws and App Store privacy declarations, before public release.
