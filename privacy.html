PRIVACY NOTICE · NOVEMBER 20, 2026

Your library. Your privacy.

This notice describes how Nightreel handles information when you create an account, build an anime library, connect with friends or send messages. Nightreel is operated as a user-created anime tracking and social application. For privacy questions, email support.nightreel@gmail.com.

Information involved in using Nightreel

  • Account information: Your email address and account identifier are used for registration, authentication and account recovery. Authentication is provided by Supabase Auth; Nightreel does not store your account password in its anime library database.
  • Profile details and visibility: Your username, display name, optional avatar and banner. You may set your profile to Public (visible to signed-in members), Friends only (visible to accepted friends), or Private (visible to you and authorized moderators reviewing safety reports). Private profiles are excluded from member search; friends-only accounts may still appear in username search so people can send friend requests.
  • Your anime collection: Titles, viewing status, episode progress, favorites, ratings, artwork and notes. Personal notes are kept separate from notes you choose to share on your anime entries. Profile visibility determines who can view your collection through Nightreel: signed-in users when Public, accepted friends when Friends only, and only you and authorized moderators when Private.
  • Social features: Friend requests and accepted connections, direct messages, blocks, safety reports, moderator decisions and appeals. Direct messages ordinarily remain visible to their participants. When someone reports a chat conversation, they intentionally select up to eight messages (including at least one received message) to share as evidence with authorized moderators. Message reports and their evidence are not automatically disclosed to the reported account. When appealing a moderation action, a user can intentionally submit up to eight selected messages from conversations they participated in. That appeal evidence is available to the appellant and authorized moderators.
  • Uploaded images: You may upload cover artwork, avatars or profile banners. Cover artwork is kept in a public storage bucket and anyone with its URL may be able to view it. Avatars and banners are stored in private buckets with access restrictions, but authorized users may receive temporary URLs to display them.
  • Technical information: Hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical information, such as IP addresses, timestamps, device/browser details, request metadata and security logs, to deliver and protect the service.

Why the information is used

Nightreel uses this information to create and protect accounts; save and synchronize anime libraries; display permitted friend activity; deliver messages; support uploaded images; investigate reports and abuse; maintain application reliability; and answer support requests.

Providers and outside sources

Nightreel uses Supabase for authentication, database storage, image storage and server functions, and GitHub Pages to host the website. Anime search and metadata can come from services such as Jikan/MyAnimeList and AniList; metadata lookups may send anime titles or identifiers to those services. Opening external anime or streaming links may subject you to those websites' privacy practices.

Who can see information

You control the visibility of your Nightreel profile and collection in My profile. Public means signed-in members can view your profile and collection; Friends only means accepted friends can view them; Private means your profile and collection are not available to other ordinary members and your account is hidden from username search. Authorized moderators retain access for reviewing safety reports, including content from private accounts. Existing friend connections and message participants may still recognize your account. Messages have participant access controls, with a limited exception for evidence a participant submits to moderators through a report or an appeal. Report details are available to their submitter and authorized moderators, but the reported user does not receive access to the reporter's private evidence simply because they were reported. Uploaded anime cover artwork is stored at public URLs, even for private collections: someone who knows a direct cover image URL may still view that image. Privacy settings do not retract URLs already shared or copies already saved by others.

Reports, appeals and safety evidence

Submitting a chat report includes only the messages the reporting participant deliberately selects from that conversation (up to eight). Authorized moderators use those snapshots to evaluate the complaint and can record actions or close the report without action. A report by itself is not a finding of wrongdoing. Reporting a conversation requires choosing Report → Select messages, selecting relevant messages, and explicitly confirming evidence submission. If an account is suspended or profile artwork removed, the affected account can submit one in-app appeal per action within 90 days, with a written explanation and up to eight voluntarily selected messages. A moderator may uphold or accept the appeal and provide a written response. Nightreel social suspensions allow a user to sign in, review existing messages and file an appeal, but block message sending and friendship changes. Users with separate sign-in problems can contact support.nightreel@gmail.com from their account email to request manual review. Evidence is not encrypted end-to-end and moderators may view the material deliberately submitted for review.

Camera, photos and permissions

Nightreel requests camera access only when you start scanning a friend's QR code. Choosing a photo or artwork for upload opens the device's file or photo picker. You can deny camera access and still use other Nightreel features.

Storage and deletion

Your information is stored while your account and relevant content remain active, subject to infrastructure, security, backup and legal retention requirements. You can edit or remove anime entries and optional images through available account features. To permanently delete your account, open Nightreel's profile menu, select Account and data deletion and follow the confirmation steps. Deletion is intended to remove account-linked library, message, friendship, profile and uploaded-image records, but technical logs, backups, and moderation evidence may persist according to database relationships, operational backups, and applicable safety or legal requirements. Report and appeal records may be deleted when associated accounts or actions are deleted; no fixed evidence retention period is currently guaranteed. Contact support if deletion fails.

Security and choices

Nightreel uses Supabase authentication, row-level database authorization and restricted storage permissions. No online service can guarantee absolute security. You can choose profile visibility, whether to add friends, share public-facing notes, upload images or grant camera access. You can block people and report concerns.

Children

Nightreel is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact support to request review and removal.

Updates and contact

This notice may be updated as Nightreel changes. The revised version will be published here with its effective date. For account, privacy, removal and safety inquiries: support.nightreel@gmail.com.

This notice describes currently inspected app behavior and should undergo final operator/legal review, particularly for retention practices, applicable privacy laws and App Store privacy declarations, before public release.
