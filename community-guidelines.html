Share the obsession.
Respect the people.

Nightreel is a place to keep track of anime and connect with friends. To keep it welcoming, everyone who creates an account or uploads content is expected to follow these rules.

Content and messages

  • No harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech or targeted abuse.
  • No sexual exploitation, nonconsensual intimate imagery, or sexual content involving minors.
  • No impersonation, scams, unwanted spam, or attempts to obtain another person’s private information.
  • Do not upload unlawful content or artwork you do not have permission or legal grounds to share.
  • Do not post someone else's personal information without their permission.

Custom artwork

You can choose your own cover image for a title. Upload only images you created or have permission or a lawful basis to share. Uploaded cover images are stored at public URLs: anyone with the image link may be able to access it, even if the associated collection isn't public. Avoid private or sensitive photographs.

Report or block a user

Open a Nightreel profile that your account is permitted to view and choose Report profile or artwork or Block user. Reports are recorded for administrator review. Blocking removes your friendship and prevents either side from sending a new friend request while the block remains in place. You can review blocked accounts in your profile menu.

Message reporting and appeals

You can report a conversation by selecting up to eight relevant messages, including at least one message you received. Only those selected messages are submitted as evidence to authorized moderators. A report alone does not prove a violation or automatically suspend an account. Suspensions restrict message sending and social actions but keep account access, existing messages, and appeals available. When an account is suspended or profile artwork is removed, the affected user may appeal within 90 days from their account menu and submit relevant messages for review. Users unable to sign in can appeal via the support email from their registered email address. Moderators may uphold or accept appeals and provide a written reason.

Support and copyright concerns

For support, privacy questions or copyright concerns, email support.nightreel@gmail.com. Please provide enough information for us to locate the content or issue.

Consequences

Content that violates these rules may be removed, and accounts may be restricted or disabled. Reports are not monitored in real time. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Your account

You can delete your Nightreel account from the profile menu, under Account and data deletion. Deletion is permanent. Use the in-app controls to manage your own content.

These are community guidelines, not a substitute for Nightreel's forthcoming formal privacy policy or terms. A formal privacy policy and any required designated copyright agent information are still being prepared for launch.
